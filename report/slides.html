The mission

  • Locked in a room; a blue door is held shut.
  • Find the key → carry it onto the green plate → the door opens.

Why it is non-trivial

  • Robot does not know where things are → it must explore & map.
  • Landmarks perceived from the camera, not queried.
  • Key is a thin 3×3 cm column → ~25 cm nav tolerance is far too coarse.
  • Monocular depth breaks at close range.

Design decisions — the “why”

frames
SLAM pose for long-range goals (no drift); odometry for short straight moves — lunge & drop backup.
stance
Perception-only: ground-truth ZMQ lookups are a baseline to remove, not a design.
mapping
Hide the carried cube from the LiDAR — no phantom obstacle in the map.
QoS
Latched target topics — a late subscriber still gets the last pose.

Limitations & future work

  • Some ground-truth shortcuts remain (ZMQ, attach/detach) → pure perception.
  • Grasp robustness on the thin column is sensitive to the lunge margin.
  • Single key / single room → multiple keys & connected rooms.
  • Only style lint today → add behavioural tests.
